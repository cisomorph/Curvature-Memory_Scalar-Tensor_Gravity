\textbf{Summary.}
The series introduces and characterises Curvature-Memory Scalar-Tensor Gravity
(\textsc{cmstg}), a covariant scalar-tensor extension of general relativity in which
a scalar field encodes retarded curvature history through a non-minimal coupling.
The Phase~1 canonical action passes all current precision cosmological tests
(Paper~II), is UV-finite to two loops in the resummed propagator (Paper~III), and
has an irreducible $2.77\sigma$ residual against DESI~Y1 BAO that is proven
structural by two no-go theorems (Paper~I). Paper~IV establishes the corresponding
dark-matter boundary: the locked action cannot produce flat galactic rotation curves
through any mechanism within the action class, proven by six independent simulation
programmes and five structural obstructions (including three Phase~5 no-go theorems
establishing that the three available extension channels are each structurally
exhausted).

\textbf{Significance.}
Together, the four papers define the dark-energy and dark-matter boundaries of a
new covariant scalar-tensor framework with strong UV properties, making concrete and
falsifiable predictions (DESI~Y3 is decisive for Paper~I; Euclid will probe the
detection threshold). The structural no-go results constitute a systematic
classification of mechanism space that is applicable to any scalar-tensor theory
with a curvature coupling, not only to \textsc{cmstg}.
