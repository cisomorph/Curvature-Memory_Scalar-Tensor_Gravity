§ 01

The setup: action, divergence, and the postulated kernel.

SIM102 / §§ 2–3

The Phase-1 CMSTG action couples a scalar Ψ non-minimally to curvature through a coupling Λ₀ ≈ 0.003 MPl⁻². A naïve cutoff gives a quartic UV divergence in the Ψ self-energy. The theory is defined by supplementing the action with a Gaussian memory kernel that exponentially suppresses hard modes — interpreted as Ψ having finite resolving power for curvature on scales below 1/km.

A quartic UV divergence.

B · Resummed propagator
Gaussian memory kernel softens UV.
Δ_Ψ(k) = exp(−k² / k_m²) / (k² + m₀²) ⇒ Σ(0) = Λ₀² k_m⁴ / (64 π²) (finite)

km is a physical memory scale — the single relevant UV scale of the theory. Universal across kernels with faster-than-power-law falloff.

§ 04

RG flow of Λ₀ and the 1 : 10⁻² : 10⁻⁶ perturbative hierarchy.

SIM105 / SIM106 · §§ 6–7

Λ₀ flows monotonically to zero in the UV.

β(Λ₀) = − Λ₀³ k_m² / (16 π² m₀²) · UV fixed point Λ₀ → 0 · no IR Landau pole

Integrating β gives 1/Λ₀² = 1/Λ₀obs² + (km² − kmnat 2)/(16π² m₀²). Fractional running over the cosmologically accessible range is just 2.47 %. Numerical SIM105 agrees with the analytic formula to better than 10⁻⁴.

Successive orders fall by factors of ≈ 40 then ≈ 7000. The expansion is stable; higher-loop corrections are negligible at any physically motivated km.

Takeaway
UV structure of CMSTG
Three independent mechanisms render CMSTG UV-finite to two loops in the resummed propagator: (i) memory damping converts power-law Ψ-loop divergences into finite functions of km; (ii) the diffeomorphism Ward identity sets Πhh(0) = 0 at every loop order, protecting the graviton mass; (iii) the derivative dI/dp²|₀ is finite in d = 4 without further regulation. The β-function drives Λ₀ to zero — General Relativity as the UV fixed point — with no Landau pole.

⚠ Caveat — finiteness here means “finite as a regulated effective theory with a physical memory scale km,” not perturbative renormalizability in the Donoghue sense. The kernel is postulated, not derived; sending km → ∞ recovers the bare quartic divergence. A first-principles derivation of km is left to future work.
