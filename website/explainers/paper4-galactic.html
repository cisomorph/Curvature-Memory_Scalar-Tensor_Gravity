01 Static field profile
SIM 99
Yukawa decays exponentially — not as r⁻².
Test: solve the static field equation around a baryonic disk

The static solution to (∇² − m²)Ψ = source falls off on the scale ~ℏ/mc. That length is microscopic compared to galactic radii, so the field is exponentially steeper than the isothermal profile a halo requires.

FAIL Profile mismatch — not a parameter problem.
02 Time-domain condensation
SIM 100
Curvature coupling is six orders too weak.
Test: 1+1D wave evolution with 2Λ₀|Rgal| source

Even in fully dynamical evolution, the curvature drive that could seed a condensate is dwarfed by the field's own restoring mass. Condensation never starts. The Gaussian memory kernel decays on τmem ≈ 200 kyr — far shorter than orbital timescales.

FAIL Drive/restoring ratio 2 × 10⁻⁶.
03 Vainshtein screening
SIM 103
Non-linear kinetic boost fails by six orders.
Test: scan Vainshtein ratio V across the full coupling space

Vainshtein-type screening can let scalar gradients amplify gravity inside a heavy source. Here the ratio V sits a million times below the screening threshold across the entire parameter scan — the non-linearity simply never switches on.

FAIL V ≤ 3.7 × 10⁻⁶ over 8 decades of Λ₀.
04 Memory-kernel halo
SIM 149 · SIM 150
Even granted perfect kernel support, the ceiling is 3× too low.
Test: ceiling on Geff/GN from accumulated memory

The accumulated Geff/GN boost from the memory kernel saturates at 1 + 2Λ₀Ψ₀² = 1.041. Forcing it up to 3.115 needs Λ₀ that violates Cassini Solar-System tests by 2 × 10⁴×.

FAIL Ceiling 1.041 vs. required 3.115.
Conclusion · § 7–8

The boundary is structural. New physics is needed — at first-principles, not as a fitted parameter.

The four mechanisms above exhaust the menu available to any massive scalar-tensor theory with curvature coupling, a memory scale, and no direct baryon–scalar term. A future galactic-scale extension of CMSTG needs:

  • Chameleon-screened mass term — environmental softening of m so the Yukawa range stretches to galactic scales while Solar-System bounds survive.
  • Coupled chameleon architecture — a direct, screened Ψ–baryon coupling absent from the locked action.
  • Sextic-stabilised condensate — higher-order self-interactions that can support a halo where the quadratic mass term cannot.
